<objective>
Discharge the two data-contract human checks against the live GitHub Pages origin: that opening an intervals.icu activity actually lazy-fetches and renders it, and that displayed dates are the activity's true local calendar date in a non-UTC timezone.

Purpose: these are the two checks that prove the fixes in plans 16-10 and 16-12 work in a real browser rather than only in vitest. The lazy-detail half of DASH-02 and must-have P07 both failed because `isValidActivityId` rejected `i`-prefixed ids before any fetch; the verifier proved over HTTP that the files themselves serve 200, so only browser-runtime behavior can close the loop. The date defect was reproduced numerically in two real timezones but its fix spans three rendering sites (list, overview, detail) that share one formatter — confirming all three needs eyes.

Output: two recorded human verdicts against `https://bacilo.github.io/strava-widgets/`.
</objective>
