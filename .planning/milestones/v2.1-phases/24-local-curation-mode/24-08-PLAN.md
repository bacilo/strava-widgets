checkpoint will be judged against from the data files rather than from the UI, then hold a
BLOCKING 14-row human browser checkpoint covering both halves of ROADMAP criterion 4.

Purpose: PROJECT.md's v2.1 milestone note records that automated gates have missed rendering
defects three times in this project, which is why every v2.1 phase ends here. This project has
also learned a sharper lesson: three clean checkpoint rounds once missed a Critical because every
row asserted only that the UI agreed with itself. So the two highest-value rows in this checkpoint
are not "did the badge appear" — they are "does the promoted next-best effort come from a
different activity, at the exact time this plan pinned before the exclusion" and "are the weekly
and monthly totals byte-for-byte the values pinned before the exclusion".

rendered evidence rather than on a frontmatter match.
