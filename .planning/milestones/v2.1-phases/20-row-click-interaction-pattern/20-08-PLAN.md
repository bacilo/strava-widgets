<house_rules>
Non-negotiable, established across checkpoints 16-09, 17-15, 19-05, 19-12, 19-17 and reinforced
by this phase's own Round 1 outcome:

- **One named verdict per row. A blanket approval covering several rows is not accepted as
  evidence in Round 2.** Round 1's blanket statement is exactly what left SC4 undischarged. If
  the developer answers several rows at once, ask again for the rows that were not individually
  described, and record any row that stays undescribed as FAIL for insufficient evidence rather
  than manufacturing detail that was never observed.
- **A theme-sensitive row observed in only one theme is FAIL for insufficient evidence**, and a
  theme-sensitive row with no stated theme at all is likewise FAIL. Rows R1, R2, R3, R4, R7, R9
  and R10 are theme-sensitive.
- **A failing row is recorded verbatim and left unpatched.** Do not fix a defect under
  checkpoint pressure, and do not record a suggested fix or a root-cause theory for a
  still-failing row - the next planning round owns diagnosis.
- **Never cite an automated result as evidence for a manual row.** `npm test` cannot observe a
  click landing, a tab stop, a focus target after navigation, or what a screen reader says.
  Plans 20-06 and 20-07 prove composed strings and stub-element focus resolution; neither proves
  a browser or VoiceOver does anything.
- **Serve under `/strava-widgets`, never at the server root.** A root-served build reproduces
  the exact class of defect that shipped Phase 16's black page behind a green gate.
- **Round 1 is a historical record.** Do not edit, reword, re-verdict or delete any Round 1 row,
  its Observation cells, its Checkpoint Outcome section or its Evidence Quality note. Round 2 is
  appended below them.
