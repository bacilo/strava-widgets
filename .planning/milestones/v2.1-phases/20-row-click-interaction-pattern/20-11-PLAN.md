<house_rules>
Non-negotiable, established across checkpoints 16-09, 17-15, 19-05, 19-12, 19-17, 20-05 and 20-08,
and reinforced by this phase's own two consecutive blanket-approval rounds:

- **One named verdict per row, and the row's own required detail with it.** A blanket statement
  covering several rows is not evidence. Round 1 and Round 2 both failed on exactly this. If the
  developer answers several rows at once, ask again naming the specific rows still undescribed;
  if a row stays undescribed after one re-ask, record it FAIL for insufficient evidence and quote
  verbatim whatever was said instead. Never manufacture detail that was not observed.
- **Rows are presented ONE AT A TIME, never as a numbered list of eighteen.** Presenting the whole
  agenda at once is what produced "all approved except 12" in Round 1 and "all pass" in Round 2.
- **A theme-sensitive row can only PASS if both the light and the dark theme are named.** Rows
  R1, R2, R3, R4, R7, R9 and R10 are theme-sensitive.
- **A failing row is recorded verbatim and left unpatched.** Do not fix a defect under checkpoint
  pressure; do not record a suggested fix or a root-cause theory for a still-failing row. The next
  planning round owns diagnosis.
- **Never cite an automated result as evidence for a manual row.** `npm test` cannot observe a
  click landing, a tab stop, a focus target after navigation, what a screen reader says, or what a
  Cmd+click does. Plan 20-09 proves a pure predicate; it proves nothing about a browser.
- **Serve under `/strava-widgets`, never at the server root.** A root-served build reproduces the
  exact class of defect that shipped Phase 16's black page behind a green gate.
- **Rounds 1 and 2 are a historical record.** Do not edit, reword, re-verdict or delete any Round 1
  or Round 2 row, Observation cell, Checkpoint Outcome section, Evidence Quality note or
  Gap-Closure Record. Round 3 is appended below all of them.
- **"I could not tell" and "I would rather not run VoiceOver" are legitimate answers**, recorded as
  insufficient evidence. An honest FAIL is more useful than an undescribed pass - that is the
  entire lesson of this phase's last two rounds.
</house_rules>
