<house_rules>
Non-negotiable, established across checkpoints 16-09, 17-15, 19-05, 19-12 and 19-17:

- **One named verdict per row.** A blanket "looks good" covering multiple rows is recorded as
  what it is (19-09's precedent) but is not manufactured into per-row detail that was never
  observed.
- **A failing row is recorded verbatim and left unpatched.** Do not fix a defect under
  checkpoint pressure. Log it as a gap with the developer's own words, and let the next
  planning round diagnose it. Do not record a suggested fix or a root-cause theory for a
  still-failing row.
- **A row observed in only one theme, where the row's claim is theme-sensitive, is FAIL for
  insufficient evidence**, not a pass. Rows 1-4, 7, 9 and 10 below are theme-sensitive.
- **Never cite an automated result as evidence for a manual row.** `npm test` being green
  cannot observe a click landing, a cursor shape, a tab stop or an announcement.
- **Serve under `/strava-widgets`, never at the server root.** A root-served build reproduces
  the exact class of defect that shipped Phase 16's black page behind a green gate.
</house_rules>
