<objective>
Close the WR-01 half of D-15 — the D-01 guard's `role` allowlist is keyed on the value being
`link`, so `role="presentation"` or `role="button"` on a `<tr>` passes undetected — and, in the
same file, pre-authorise the two source shapes plan 20-17's D-13 work will introduce, so that plan
can land without a guard fighting it.

Purpose: `20-REVIEW.md` WR-01 proved by execution that five distinct role mutations pass the
current guard, three of which put a role on a row element and defeat D-01 outright. The harm D-01
exists to prevent, stated in `row-navigation.ts:10-13`, is that *any* role on a `<tr>` removes it
from the table's accessibility tree — `presentation` is strictly worse than `link`, since it strips
the row with no replacement semantic at all. The rule as written is wider than the invariant it
claims to enforce, and the eleven self-tests only ever exercise the value `link`, so nothing
surfaces it.

The second half is sequencing, not scope: D-13 gives every non-Date cell of both Records tables a
real anchor carrying `tabIndex = -1`, and moves the Flags badges inside that cell's anchor. Both
shapes trip guards in this file today. Widening them here — receiver-named and comment-justified,
and green both before and after — is what lets plan 20-17 land the anchors in a later wave without
either plan being red in between.

Output: a receiver-keyed role rule with proof, widened value patterns, one named new tabIndex
receiver, and a records guard written to tolerate exactly the D-13 shape and nothing looser.
</objective>

   `dateAnchor`.

to a wildcard, a suffix match, or "any identifier ending in Anchor".
