<house_rules>
Non-negotiable, established across checkpoints 16-09, 17-15, 19-05, 19-12, 19-17, 20-05, 20-08 and
20-11:

- **One named verdict per row, and the row's own required detail with it.** A blanket statement
  covering several rows is not evidence. Rounds 1 and 2 both failed on exactly this; Round 3 was
  the first round to produce genuine per-row evidence and its convention is kept verbatim.
- **Rows are presented ONE AT A TIME**, never as a numbered list of twelve.
- **Every row names its observer**, in the Observation cell, using one of exactly three phrasings:
  "observed by developer", "observed by agent (browser automation against the staged build)", or
  "observed by developer, corroborated by agent". Round 3 introduced this attribution and
  `20-VERIFICATION.md` weighed it explicitly; do not blur it.
- **Rows marked "Observer required: developer's own eyes" cannot be answered by agent
  automation.** If the developer declines, the row is recorded BLOCKED with the decline quoted —
  not settled by a computed-style read. That substitution is the specific caveat the verification
  raised against Round 3's R7 and R9.
- **A theme-sensitive row can only PASS if both the light and the dark theme are named.**
- **A failing row is recorded verbatim and left unpatched.** No suggested fix, no root-cause theory
  for a still-failing row. The next planning round owns diagnosis.
- **Never cite an automated result as evidence for a manual row.** `npm test` cannot observe a
  click landing, a tab stop, a stolen focus, an underline, or what a Cmd+click does.
- **Serve under `/strava-widgets`, never at the server root.**
- **Rounds 1, 2 and 3 are a historical record.** Do not edit, reword, re-verdict or delete any of
  their rows, Observation cells, Checkpoint Outcome sections, Evidence Quality notes or Gap-Closure
  Records. Round 4 is appended below all of them.
- **The four-state verdict vocabulary from Round 3 is kept:** PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED, NOT EXERCISABLE.
  BLOCKED means the observation could not be produced for a dataset or environment reason;
  NOT EXERCISABLE means the gesture itself could not be performed. Neither is a defect verdict.
- **"I could not tell" and "I would rather not" are legitimate answers**, recorded as they were
  given.
