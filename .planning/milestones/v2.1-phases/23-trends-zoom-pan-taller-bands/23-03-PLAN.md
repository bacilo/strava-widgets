<objective>
Give all three zoomable Trends tabs the `.chart-band` wrapper markup, applied by one shared
helper, and put every Trends band on the D-19 tall height.

Purpose: this is prerequisite structural work that none of the 25 decisions anticipated, found by
reading the source rather than trusting CONTEXT.md. Only `buildChannelBand`
(`trends-charts.ts:334-353`, Cadence & HR) builds `.chart-band` / `.chart-band__header` /
`.chart-band__canvas-wrap` markup today. The Volume tab (`trends.ts:601-603`) and the Training
Load tab (`trends.ts:899-901`) each create a bare, unclassed `<div>` - which means those two
charts currently get NO `.chart-band__canvas-wrap` height rule at all, and D-10's control cluster
and D-18/D-19's tall modifier have nowhere to attach on two of the three tabs this phase targets.

Without this plan, plans 23-04 and 23-05 would each have to invent their own wrapper, and TRN-03
would be discharged on one tab out of three.

Output: an exported `buildChartBand` helper in `trends-charts.ts`, `buildChannelBand` refactored
onto it, and both `trends.ts` call sites converted.
</objective>
