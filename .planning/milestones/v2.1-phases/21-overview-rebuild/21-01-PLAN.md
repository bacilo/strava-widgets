<objective>
Close FIX-01's producer half: make the streak's end day a real, named, unconditionally-populated
field, and make the Records view-logic layer read that field instead of misreading
`currentStreakStart`.

Purpose: D-12 established the defect is two-layered. `streak-utils.ts:118` nulls
`currentStreakStart` exactly when a streak has ended, and `records-logic.ts:274-282` then maps that
same `currentStreakStart` onto `endedISO`. Fixing only the first layer would ship a sub-label that
renders, looks plausible, and names the streak's **start** date under an "ended" label — a
confidently wrong date that passes a does-it-render check. Both layers land in this one plan so no
wave can leave layer 1 shipped alone.

Output: `currentStreakEnd` flowing from `calculateDailyStreaks` through `StreakData` and
`compute-advanced-stats` into `streaks.json`, and `selectCurrentStreak` reading it — with the
Records screen's already-correct `records.ts:291-303` sub-label needing no DOM change at all.
</objective>
