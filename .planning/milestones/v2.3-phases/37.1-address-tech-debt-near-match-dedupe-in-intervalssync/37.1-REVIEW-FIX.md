### WR-01: Tie-break depends on directory order and disagrees with reconcile's "earlier wins"

**Files modified:** `src/exports/near-match.ts`, `src/exports/near-match.test.ts`
**Commit:** cc102646
**Status:** fixed: requires human verification (logic change)
**Applied fix:** When two candidates are equally close, `findNearMatch` now picks the one with the earlier epoch, which is reconcile's rule. ### WR-02: reconcile.ts keeps its own copy of the window comparison

**Files modified:** `src/exports/reconcile.ts`, `src/exports/reconcile.test.ts`, `src/exports/near-match.test.ts`
**Commit:** 9e15cbb0
**Status:** fixed: requires human verification (logic change)
**Applied fix:** I used the first option. reconcile keeps its `lowerBound` lookup, so it stays fast. It now passes the two neighbouring archive epochs to `findNearMatch(run.startEpoch, neighbours, tolerance)`. The window, the signed `deltaS` and the tie-break therefore all come from near-match.ts.
