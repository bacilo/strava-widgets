## Blocking Developer Decision

**Family G (the stationary/walked speed boundary, D-07, which also ships as STOP-02's near-zero threshold) cannot ship from this artifact as written — a second time, after a different re-declaration.** Per this plan's own halt protocol (mirroring the 33-08/32-05/34-06-HALT-1 precedent), no shape, order, tolerance, margin or plant rule was widened, loosened, reordered or hand-picked.

**What the numbers show.** G4 and G5's population — chained standstill episodes (two or more zero-advance pauses joined by a bridge under `P_a`) — is extremely rare on this archive: **6 chained episodes out of 684 total standstill episodes archive-wide (chainedShare 0.8772%)**. 678 of 684 standstills are single, isolated pauses with no qualifying bridge to another pause at all. This tiny population fails D-13 on **both** axes, by wide margins, for two distinct measured reasons:

1. **REMOVED axis (89.7455% delta, both G4 and G5).** Dropping episodes near any J-interval neighbourhood empties 2 of the archive's 6 chained episodes (6 → 4). This is not a plant-design artifact — it is the archive's own composition, and it is exactly what 34-12-PLAN.md's own disclosed caveat (c5) predicted in advance: *"Small population: at most half the archive's pause intervals. Pauses often adjoin recording gaps (auto-pause after a stop), so the REMOVED view may drop many episodes. That is a real sensitivity for the harness to measure, not a defect."* The measurement confirms the caveat.
2. **PLANTED axis (3902.09% / 1561.89% delta, G4 / G5).** With only 6 real items, the proportional plant count (`ceil(0.01 x 6) = 1`) is unavoidable — D-19's own counting rule guarantees at least one plant on any non-empty population — but a single planted item, cloned from a real J-interval speed (~0.9-1.9 m/s, the transport/walking scale) into a population whose real rates sit near 0.03 m/s (the drift scale), is 30-60x larger than a typical real item. On a population of 6, one such outsized item moves the R-7 p90 by orders of magnitude. This is a **scale mismatch between the plant's source population (J, transport speeds) and the target population's own scale (chained-episode drift rates)**, compounded by the target population's tiny size — not a bug in the D-19 proportional-planting mechanism itself, which is confirmed working correctly (every row's `planted items = base items + plants`, verified above, and 34-12's own 95 synthetic tests already prove the mechanism in both directions).

**Three measured, named options — none chosen here:**

1. **A different, larger population for Family G that does not require a "chained" (>=2-pause) qualification.** G4/G5's own REJECTED-ON-ARGUMENT list already considered and rejected "(f) chained plus single-pause standstills together" (a single-pause standstill accrues exactly 0 by construction, risking a zero base) and "(c) pause-interval interiors alone" (same zero-base risk). Both rejections are sound as argued. A shape that folds in single-pause standstills' *adjoining* covered-pair drift (rather than the zero-advance pause interval's own span) might reach a much larger population (up to 678 additional standstills) without inheriting the zero-base problem — this needs its own non-circularity argument, which is a re-planning decision, not this plan's to make.
2. **A different bridging/chaining criterion.** The current chain requires a bridge whose real-time span is strictly less than `P_a` between two pauses. Loosening or restating this criterion (still argued from first principles, still speed-free) could turn some of the 678 single-pause standstills into chained episodes, directly enlarging G4/G5's population — investigating *why* chaining is this rare (whether `P_a`'s own scale, ~5x the activity's p90 advance interval, is simply too strict a bridge-duration bound for how sparsely this archive's gaps/segments are distributed around real stops) is itself a measured finding worth pursuing before any redesign.
3. **A third full re-declaration of Family G**, since two independent population designs have now failed D-13 for two different, disclosed, archive-specific structural reasons (G1/G2/G3: measured walking/jogging speeds instead of drift; G4/G5: a population too small and too differently-scaled for the plant mechanism to test meaningfully). This does not imply the drift constant is unmeasurable — it implies the population definition needs a third attempt, informed by both prior failures' disclosed caveats.

**Options explicitly not available**, per D-19's own developer decision (2026-09-22) and this plan's own halt protocol: widening the fixed 1% `INSENSITIVITY_TOLERANCE`, changing the `PLANT_FRACTION` formula, falling back to a later/rejected shape, or picking a value by looking at the measured deltas.

**This plan's own generator (built in plan 34-04, amended in plan 34-12), its 95 synthetic tests, and this artifact are complete, committed and correct as specified — the HALT is the measured, honest output of running them against the real archive a second time, not an implementation gap.** Plans 34-07 through 34-11 remain blocked on this decision, since none of PAUSE-01's four classes or STOP-02's threshold exist without Family G's constant.
