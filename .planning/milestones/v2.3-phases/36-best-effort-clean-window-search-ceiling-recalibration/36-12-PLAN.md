THE DIFFERENT ALGORITHM, per `36-RESEARCH.md`'s recommendation and its Assumption A2: a per-window
SELF-REFERENTIAL outlier statistic. For the window an effort names, compute each sample-to-sample pair's
implied speed `(d[i+1] - d[i]) / (t[i+1] - t[i])` over the pairs inside the window, then the window's own
outlier ratio `maxPairSpeed / medianPairSpeed`. This is deliberately coarser than the detector: it does
not attempt to reproduce the detector's boundary, only to confirm something anomalous really is inside
the window. It must not import or retype `UNRELIABLE_SPEED_LIMIT_MPS`, `excessFloorMetres` or
`hiddenPauseMinMetres`, and must not compute `speedLimitMps * seconds` — that literal is banned outside
its two permitted files by `pace-single-source.test.ts`'s `DETECTOR_ARITHMETIC_LITERALS`.

⚠ THE VERDICT THRESHOLD IS A DECLARED VALUE WITH A MEASURED BASIS, NOT A GUESS. A hand-picked ratio can
be unpassable (too high — the real bursts do not clear it) or unfailable (too low — every window clears
it). Both failure modes have shipped on this milestone. The basis must be MEASURED FIRST inside the same
run: a CONTROL population of ranked windows with `contaminated === null`, whose outlier ratios establish
what a genuinely clean window looks like on this archive. Task 1 measures both distributions before
anything is declared.
