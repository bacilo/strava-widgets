<objective>
Make a best-effort window unable to span, or interpolate a crossing across, an unreliable segment —
by partitioning the stream, never by splicing it.

Purpose: this is the phase. Everything else records, surfaces or verifies what this plan computes.
Three things make it delicate and all three are already-shipped failures elsewhere in this codebase:
a second boundary convention silently dropping metres (32-13/32-17, fixed twice); a fourth guard
attached to the old duration silently dropping a genuine clean time out of the ceiling's population
(`compute-best-efforts.ts:304`); and a "clean" span that means something narrower in best efforts
than it already means in the pace chart, histogram and coverage caption.

Output: the partition, the search, the two record branches, the one reason builder, and the caller
wiring that makes the detector load-bearing here.

Requirement-tick safety: this plan ticks nothing and edits no planning document. It must NOT touch the
SEG-05b row — plan 36-14 records Phase 36's half. Skip any end-of-plan `requirements mark-complete`
call.
