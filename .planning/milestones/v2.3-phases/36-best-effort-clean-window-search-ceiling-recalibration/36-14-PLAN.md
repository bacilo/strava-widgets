  <what-built>
    A best-effort window can no longer span, or interpolate a crossing across, an unreliable segment. The
    search now partitions the stream at the detector's own merged segment spans and takes the best window
    inside the clean complement, reusing the one existing two-pointer sweep under an index bound rather
    than deleting samples and re-stitching the array. Where a clean window exists the effort ranks on it
    and carries a new additive `contaminated` record holding the original window, its pace and a reason
    naming the segments it crossed with their measured metres and seconds. Where no clean window covers the
    distance the effort is kept, carrying the contaminated figure, demoted under a new
    `unreliable-segment` guard whose reason also states how far the longest clean stretch reached. The
    absolute guards and the personal ceiling now only ever see the clean figure, which means some efforts
    those guards had rejected are reinstated. Because contaminated timings no longer enter the ceiling's
    p90 population, every distance's ceiling moved: most fell, one rose, and one lost its ceiling entirely
    by falling below the population floor and failing open to the world-record and max-speed guards alone.
    On the activity detail view a moved effort now carries its own badge, visually distinct from the
    demoted badge, naming the original figure's reason without leaving the view. On the Records screen the
    new guard is counted by name rather than as "another guard", and a second note discloses how many
    efforts at each distance now rank on a clean window. The committed ceiling-state file can no longer be
    rewritten by an ordinary local command; only the nightly CI invocation opts in. Two generated,
    regenerable artifacts record how the ceiling moved and what changed archive-wide, and a third,
    import-free recount checks the claim independently. Nothing about distance, totals, moving time, splits
    or pace changed anywhere, and the review queue was not touched.
