Merge origin, regenerate the signed artifacts on the merged tree, prove the whole gate green on a
digest-verified build, and freeze every checkpoint row against a value derived outside the changed code.

Purpose: PR-09's signature is a sha256 over a specific archive snapshot, and this repository's nightly CI
moves `origin/master` while a phase runs — v2.2 ran 402 commits ahead and the archive shifted under two
calibration artifacts, which is why MERGE-01 exists. A signature taken before the merge signs a snapshot
that no longer exists. Separately, the developer must judge rendered claims against numbers, not
impressions, which means every row's Expected value is frozen here, before the round, and derived
somewhere other than the code being judged.

`36-VALIDATION.md` whose every row is bound and frozen.

`node_modules` and a writable `data/`, and its fixture failures are not regressions.

Traceability table, the SEG-05b row, the ROADMAP Phase 36 checkbox, the progress table or STATE.md. Plan
36-14 alone sets dispositions. Skip any end-of-plan `requirements mark-complete` call.
