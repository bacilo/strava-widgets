  <behavior>
    - Fast step: a step is fast iff `metres > speedLimitMps * seconds` (multiplication, no division, so there is no divide-by-zero)
    - Run: a maximal sequence of consecutive fast steps where each step's startIndex equals the previous step's endIndex (contiguous). A gap pair or a non-fast step ends the run
    - Burst: a run whose aggregate excess `metres - speedLimitMps * seconds` (metres = d[run.endIndex] - d[run.startIndex], seconds = t[run.endIndex] - t[run.startIndex]) is strictly greater than excessFloorMetres. A run at or below the floor is not a burst and yields nothing (D-09)
    - Gap block: each input gap span mapped to sample indices [gi, gj] with t[gi] === startSec and t[gj] === endSec. Use the last index with t === startSec for gi and the first index with t === endSec for gj; a span whose bounds match no sample time is malformed
    - Touching (D-06): a burst touches a gap iff burst.endIndex === gap.startIndex (leading) or burst.startIndex === gap.endIndex (trailing). There is no tolerance and no clean steps are allowed in between
    - Chaining (D-07): order all bursts and gap blocks by startIndex. A maximal sequence in which each element's startIndex equals the previous element's endIndex AND which contains at least one gap AND at least one burst becomes ONE gap-adjacent segment. A gap block touching no burst yields nothing (it stays a plain gap for Phase 33). Two blocks separated by even one clean step stay separate
    - Segment fields: startIndex/endIndex of the chain, startSec/endSec, metres = d[endIndex]-d[startIndex], seconds = t[endIndex]-t[startIndex], parts in time order (kind 'burst' or 'gap', each with its own endpoint-difference metres and seconds), burstMetres/burstSeconds/gapMetres/gapSeconds = sums over parts, burstCount/gapCount
    - Mechanism (D-03/D-08): gapCount > 0 → 'gap-adjacent'; otherwise metres >= hiddenPauseMinMetres → 'hidden-pause', else 'isolated-jump'
    - Leading-side case: [clean, clean, fast, fast | gap | clean] gives one segment of burstCount 1, gapCount 1 whose first part is the burst
    - Trailing-side case: [clean | gap | fast, clean] gives the gap then the burst
    - Both sides: [fast | gap | fast] gives ONE segment with parts burst, gap, burst
    - Chain: [fast | gapA | fast | gapB | clean] gives ONE segment with gapCount 2, burstCount 2
    - Not swallowed: [fast | gapA | fast, CLEAN, fast | gapB] gives TWO segments
    - Near miss: [fast, CLEAN | gap] gives a no-gap segment (isolated-jump or hidden-pause by metres), not gap-adjacent
    - Diluted slew: five consecutive 1 s steps at 9 m/s each with a limit of 6 and a floor of 5 give one run with excess 15 > 5, so a segment; each step is individually under 10.44 m/s
    - Aliasing non-fire: a full/zero alternation that begins with a zero pair, with folded speed 4 m/s (each advancing pair 8 m over 1 s, per-pair 8 m/s) and limit 6, gives []. Per-pair scoring would have fired; folded run-level scoring does not
    - Floor: a single fast step with excess 4 and floor 5 gives []; with floor 3 it gives one isolated-jump
    - Conservation: for every segment in every test, metres === burstMetres + gapMetres within 1e-9 and seconds === burstSeconds + gapSeconds within 1e-9, and parts are contiguous (each part.startIndex === previous part.endIndex)
    - Guards: invalid t/d → []; params with NaN, Infinity or a value <= 0 for speedLimitMps, or a negative/non-finite excessFloorMetres or hiddenPauseMinMetres → []; a gap span with non-finite bounds, endSec <= startSec, or bounds matching no sample time → []. None of these throw
