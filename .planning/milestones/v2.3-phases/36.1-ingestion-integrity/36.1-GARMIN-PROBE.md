## §4 Units

- Start time is epoch milliseconds. Evidence: `startTimeGmt` 1794983150000 maps to 2026-11-18T06:25:50Z; the last run-like row maps to 2026-11-24T06:34:17Z.
- Distance is centimetres. Method (a), the 2026-04-20 comparison against archive 18180779941 (10012.5 m), is not computable because the export has no row that day (H2). Replacement independent checks:
  - Row 25116720114 against its original FIT's final record distance (12609.7 m, decoded by `readFit`): raw distance / 12609.7 = 100.0000.
  - All 1048 run-like rows whose epoch matches an archive record exactly: median of `distance/100 / archive.distance` = 1.0000 (min 0.917, max 16.03, so a few rows differ in what they measure; the median is the unit signal).
- Method (b): median over run-like rows of `distance / (avgSpeed * duration)` = approximately 1.0000. So `avgSpeed` is in centimetres per millisecond, which is metres per second divided by 10 (row 25116720114: 1260970 cm over 4520169 ms is 2.79 m/s, and the sample row's avgSpeed is 0.2752 in these units, i.e. 2.75 m/s). Duration is milliseconds (row 25116720114: 4520169 ms = 4520 s, consistent with the FIT).

### H2 resolution

Per L-01a, archive record 18180779941 (2026-04-20) is a Strava-only recording made on the phone as a backup, because the watch was not charged that day. The Garmin export cannot contain it, and the Garmin audit correctly does not cover it. The direction matters: `missing` lists export runs absent from the archive, so an archive-only record can never appear there. Any check of the form "18180779941 is not listed as missing" is vacuous against a Garmin export, and the 2026-04-20 twin rows are therefore dropped from the fixture and from 36.1-08 and 36.1-09. The both-present row is G2 above. The 4 extra missing runs (below) are listed in the audit only and not imported.
