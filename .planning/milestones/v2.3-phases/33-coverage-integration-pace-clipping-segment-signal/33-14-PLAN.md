(the planner ran only a counts-and-booleans existence probe: all four reference populations exist
over 1,873 activities with at least one folded covered step / 2,624,615 pooled steps, none is

Common definitions (all shapes):
  - A "view" of an activity is `{ id, steps, totalDistanceM, excessList }`. In the BASE view,
    `steps` = `advanceSteps(t, d, gaps)` from `dist/analytics/unreliable-segments.js` with
    `gaps = classifyGapsThreeWay(t, d).gapIntervals`; `totalDistanceM = d[n-1] - d[0]`;
    `excessList` = each detected segment's own `excessMetres` at `DEFAULT_DETECTOR_PARAMS`.
  - Activities with `totalDistanceM <= 0` or zero steps contribute to no reference population and
    are counted and reported, never coerced to 0.
  - Every percentile is the ONE imported R-7 `quantile` over a sorted-ascending copy.
  - No shape introduces a new free percentile order: 0.90 is 32-CONTEXT.md D-10(a)'s locked outer
    order, 0.95 and 0.50 are the inner orders `statisticSA` / `speedLimitMultiplierAt` already use.
  - The cut each shape yields is applied identically: `severe` iff the activity's excess fraction
    is STRICTLY GREATER than the cut; `minor` iff it has at least one segment and is not severe;
    `none` iff it has zero segments (33-08's planner decision, unchanged).

ORDER (fixed; criteria, in priority: (1) the reference's unit matches the judged unit - an
ACTIVITY-level distance-error scale, since the judged quantity is an activity-level aggregate;
(2) independence from the detector and its constants; (3) fewest inherited caveats):

R1 - "accumulated ordinary step noise".
  Reference population: ALL computable activities (flagged or not), one value each.
  Statistic: for activity a, take every INTERIOR step i whose two neighbours are index-contiguous
  (`steps[i-1].endIndex === steps[i].startIndex` and `steps[i].endIndex === steps[i+1].startIndex`);
  with `v = metres / seconds`, residual metres `r_i = |v_i - (v_(i-1) + v_(i+1)) / 2| * seconds_i`;
  `sigma_a = 1.4826 * quantile(sorted r, 0.5) * sqrt(2/3)` (1.4826 is the normal-consistency factor
  of a median absolute deviation and sqrt(2/3) undoes the 3/2 variance inflation of a
  centred-second-difference - mathematical identities of the stated model, not tunables);
  `epsilon_a = sigma_a * sqrt(steps.length) / totalDistanceM`. An activity with no interior residual
  is excluded and counted. Cut = R-7 p90 across activities of `epsilon_a`.
  Meaning: the share of its own distance that an ordinary activity's sample-to-sample distance noise
  could plausibly accumulate to. Severe = the impossible excess is larger than that.
  Disclosed model assumption: independent per-step errors accumulating as a random walk. The
  artifact must state this assumption in words.
  Non-circularity argument: `sigma_a` is a MEDIAN over thousands of ordinary residuals per activity
  (50% breakdown point), so the handful of burst steps a flagged activity carries cannot move it;
  the across-activity p90 is taken over every activity, 63% of which carry no segment at all; and
  nothing in the statistic references the speed limit, the floor, a segment, or the flagged set.
  Removing detected bursts changes `steps.length` and `totalDistanceM` by fractions of a percent;
  planting one burst into an activity adds one non-contiguous step that enters no residual.

R2 - "own-distance step share, bulk-of-bulk" (the S-A shape with speed replaced by distance share).
  Reference population: ALL computable activities, one value each.
  Statistic: per activity, R-7 p95 of `step.metres / totalDistanceM` over its own steps; cut = R-7
  p90 across activities of those per-activity values.
  Meaning: the share of its own activity that one high-ordinary recorded step carries - the
  granularity at which the stream resolves distance at all. Severe = the impossible excess exceeds
  what a single ordinary sample step could ever account for.
  Disclosed caveat: this is a SAMPLE-level scale, the same population `UNRELIABLE_EXCESS_FLOOR_M`
  was drawn from (its p10), so it sits closer to a detection scale than R1 does - which is why it
  ranks below R1 on criterion (1).
  Non-circularity argument: the population is every ordinary step of every activity (the population
  `statisticSA` and `deriveExcessFloor` both already proved bulk-dominated); a per-activity p95 over
  thousands of steps shifts by a rank or two when a few burst steps leave or arrive; no detector
  constant and no flagged membership enters it.

R3 - "bulk step over bulk distance" (R2's reference idea with the two scales decoupled).
  Statistic: `quantile(all steps' metres pooled archive-wide, 0.90) / quantile(all activities'
  totalDistanceM, 0.50)`.
  Meaning: one high-ordinary step as a share of a typical-length activity.
  Ranks below R2 because it judges every activity against a TYPICAL distance rather than through
  each activity's own distance (D-07's framing), but its two factors are each a bulk statistic of a
  population the detector barely touches.
  Non-circularity argument: the numerator is the very population `deriveExcessFloor` reads, measured
  at 0.00% / 0.00% under this same removal-and-planting harness in 32-CALIBRATION.md; the
  denominator is a median over all activities of a quantity segments alter by well under 1%.

R4 - "hidden-pause boundary over bulk distance".
  Statistic: `HIDDEN_PAUSE_MIN_METRES / quantile(all activities' totalDistanceM, 0.50)`, reading the
  constant from `DEFAULT_DETECTOR_PARAMS.hiddenPauseMinMetres`, never a literal.
  Meaning: the share of a typical activity that a pause-scale distance lie represents.
  Ranks LAST on criteria (2) and (3): its numerator is a DETECTOR constant whose own derivation
  found no clean valley and fell back to the midpoint of an anchor interval whose upper end is
  3475734189's own segment, so (a) its D-13 insensitivity is partly insensitivity-of-a-constant,
  weak evidence, and (b) the D-07 anchor check on 3475734189 is not independent of it; and it
  compares an excess-metres quantity against a segment-metres scale. The artifact must state all
  three caveats if R4 is the shape that ships.
  Non-circularity argument: neither factor is a statistic of the flagged cohort's excess fractions;
  the denominator is R3's.

    a detected segment (`step.startIndex >= part.startIndex && step.endIndex <= part.endIndex` -

    `excessList` (every detected segment is gone).

    **3475724402** at `DEFAULT_DETECTOR_PARAMS` (real shapes, no synthetic look-alikes); clone them
    round-robin into the activities sorted by id until the planted count equals the archive-wide
    detected SEGMENT count (at least doubling the contamination); each plant is appended as a step
    with negative sentinel indices (never index-contiguous with a real step), its metres are added
    to `totalDistanceM`, and its own burst-level excess (metres minus the speed limit times seconds,
    counted only when above the excess floor) is appended to that activity's `excessList`.
  - Denominators are RE-DERIVED on the perturbed views, never held fixed: a real slew inflates an
    activity's own distance, and shielding the statistic from that route would be a laxer check.
  - delta = |perturbed - base| / |base| on RAW values; a null or zero base, or a null perturbed
    value (an emptied population), is verdict FAIL - an emptied population is the most extreme
    sensitivity there is, never a pass.
  - Non-vacuity, asserted BEFORE any delta is trusted and rendered in the artifact: removed step
    count > 0, planted count >= detected segment count > 0, source burst shape count > 0, and the
    number of activities whose `totalDistanceM` changed under each perturbation.
  - STRAWMAN in this harness: R-7 q=0.99 of the excess fractions (`sum(excessList) /
    totalDistanceM`) of activities with a non-empty `excessList`. Its BASE value must equal 33-08's
    legacy q=0.99 cut exactly (same population, same quantile) - assert it; a mismatch means the
    views are wrong.
