<objective>
Close Finding 9, the sole cause of R15's FAIL and the only thing keeping TRN-03 Pending.

R15 recorded that D-21's band floor holds exactly at every required phone width — 390→240px,
393→240px, 412→240px, 430→240px, each `30dvh` of an 800px viewport and inside
`clamp(160px, 30dvh, 260px)` — and that the chart canvas reflows correctly at every one of them
(260/263/282/300 px wide inside 294/297/316/334 px columns). Only the row's non-waivable
"confirm there is no horizontal overflow" clause failed, and it failed for one reason:
`.year-heatmap` measured `scrollWidth` **634** at every viewport (634 at 390, 393, 412, 430 and 500
alike), pinning `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` at **682**.

634 is not a mystery number. `.year-heatmap` (`styles.css:1441`) is
`grid-template-columns: repeat(53, 1fr)` with `gap: 2px`, and `.year-heatmap__cell` declares
`min-width: 10px`. A `1fr` track is `minmax(auto, 1fr)`, so each track's floor is the cell's
min-content contribution of 10px: 53 × 10 + 52 × 2 = **634px** exactly. The grid cannot reflow
below that at any viewport, by construction.

Purpose: R15 is TRN-03's only blocking row.

Output: one new CSS rule, one `className` assignment, by-value tests, and three stale comments
corrected in the same change.
</objective>
