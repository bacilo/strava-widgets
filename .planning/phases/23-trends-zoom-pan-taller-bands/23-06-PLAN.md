<objective>
Make the Volume granularity toggle reset the zoom (D-23), and convert the Training Load window
control from a dataset slicer into a set of zoom presets over one always-complete series (D-03).

Purpose: D-03 is one of the two decisions that deliberately exceed the requirements' literal
wording, and it was taken knowingly - a verifier should expect to see a shipped Phase 18 control
change meaning, and not score it as drift. Its point is that "All + zoomed to 3 months" and "3mo"
stop being two paths to one picture with different state. Leaving `sliceLoadWindow` in place as an
unused export would leave the door open for exactly the mechanism D-03 removed, so it is retired
rather than parked.

D-23 exists because carrying a three-month zoom into "Yearly" shows three bars, and carrying a
fifteen-year view into "Weekly" recreates the exact compression TRN-01 exists to fix.

Output: `trends.ts`'s two control handlers rewritten, `sliceLoadWindow` deleted from the logic
module and its tests.
</objective>
