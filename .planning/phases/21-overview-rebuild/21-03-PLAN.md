<objective>
Give D-06's two-line row its layout, in a Phase 21 banner-commented block, without disturbing any
of the bordered-card values D-08 froze.

Purpose: today `.activity-row` is `display: flex; flex-direction: row; flex-wrap: wrap`, which lays
the name, the meta line and every badge span out as flat siblings that wrap unpredictably. That
`flex-wrap: wrap` is the literal mechanism producing OVR-01's "three stacked divs" complaint. Plan
21-02 gave the row a header wrapper and a badges wrapper; this plan makes them lay out.

The stylesheet is the single source of design tokens (16-D04) and is never imported from
TypeScript, so the class contract this block states is the only coupling between the two plans.

Output: two new rules for the new wrappers, two property edits on the existing `.activity-row`
rule, and the cascade-aware assertions that pin all of it.
</objective>
