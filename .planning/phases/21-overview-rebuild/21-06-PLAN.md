<objective>
Add this-year distance and hours to Headline Stats, and give Overview's Current Streak tile the
`ended {date}` sub-label the Records tile already has.

Purpose: OVR-04 is the one Headline Stats gap — the card shows four all-time totals and two streak
figures, and nothing scoped to the year the reader is actually in. D-10 established there is no
pipeline work to do: `yearly-stats.json` is already computed, already published by
`copyDataFiles`, and already fetched by `trends.ts:1167` with the identical idiom.

D-15 pulls the second half of FIX-01 onto this screen. Without it, after plan 21-01 the Records
Current Streak tile would explain its zero while Overview's showed a bare unexplained `0 days` —
on the very screen this phase exists to fix. `buildStatCard` gains the optional third parameter
`buildSuperlativeTile` (`records.ts:224`) has had all along.

Output: an eight-tile grid, one more guarded fetch, and three small exported pure functions that
make every rendered value assertable without a DOM.
