<objective>
Build the three altitude detectors, their shared elevation signal shape, and the three synthetic
per-mode fixtures that make mode independence provable — then land elevation as the sixth signal
on `ActivityQualitySignals` without disturbing the `anySevere` composite.

Purpose: this plan is the whole mechanism half of ELEV-01. Every later plan in this phase (index
row, badges, detail rows, calibration report, recount, checkpoint) reads what this plan defines.
D-06 is the decision most easily reversed by accident, so the structural guarantee that keeps
elevation out of `anySevere` — and its test — lands here, beside the code it constrains.

Output: three pure detectors, one `ElevationSignal` per activity, three single-mode synthetic
fixtures, and a green `tsc --noEmit` across a tree where `ActivityQualitySignals` just grew a
required key.
