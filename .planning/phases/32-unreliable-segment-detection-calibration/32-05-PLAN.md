<objective>
Measure, on the real named inputs, whether D-10's constraint (d) can be satisfied: flag every named
burst on the three scoping activities AND spare D-05's fixtures. Measure the D-05 candidates at the
detector's own judging scale and the candidate speed-limit statistics with their D-13
insensitivity. Then stop for a blocking developer decision before any constant is derived.

Purpose: during planning, a throwaway reachability probe (uncalibrated, per-sample folded steps,
references only, NOT expected values) found that D-10(d) looks UNREACHABLE with the research's
proposed D-05 fixtures. Inside `16628769295`'s corroborated span there is a 29.4 m / 2 s pair
(t=1032), and inside `8869338724`'s a 29.2 m / 2 s pair (t=3004). At every limit scanned between
7 and 10 m/s, those runs carried more excess metres than the smallest named jumps on 3475740390
(t=2347, 30.1 m / 3 s; t=2580). With the absolute-limit + excess-floor rule shape D-09/D-10 lock,
no floor then admits all five jumps and spares those two fixtures. `7409028052` was spared. The
research vetted the fixtures at aggregate scale (24-33 s), not at the scale the detector judges.
RESEARCH.md also left the D-05 IDs "not yet locked, pending confirmation" and K's measured method
open (Open Question 3). Both need a developer decision, and the lessons require a reachability
problem to be reported honestly, not engineered around.

This plan builds the generator skeleton so the evidence is regenerable and committed, then presents
it. It must run on the PRIMARY checkout: it has a checkpoint and reads the whole archive.

Output: generator + test (pre-calibration functions), npm scripts for the calibration and the
recount, the first generated `32-CALIBRATION.md`, and the recorded decision.
</objective>
