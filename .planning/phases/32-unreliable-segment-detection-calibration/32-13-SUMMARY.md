## Decisions Made

- Chose FIRST-at-startSec/LAST-at-endSec (widening the gap block on both ends) over narrowing
  `isGapPair` instead, per the plan's interface notes: widening can only ever ADD a duplicate
  sample's own zero-duration, zero-distance contribution to the gap side, never move metres
  between burst and gap tallies; narrowing `isGapPair` would reclassify pairs `advanceSteps`
  currently discards as covered steps, changing folded-step output archive-wide.
- Renamed the "A and B agree..." fixture title to drop the word "mechanism" after discovering it
  collided with the `-t "mechanism"` filter (Rule 1 — my own test's naming bug, not a 32-08
  fixture), restoring that filter's expected 12-match count exactly.
