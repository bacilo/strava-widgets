<objective>
Record how the ceiling moved, as a generated figure of record the developer can sign.

Purpose: `deriveCeiling`'s p90 is population-sensitive by design, so moving contaminated timings out of
the population moves every distance's ceiling at once — and the probe measured one distance moving UP
while the rest fell, and one losing its ceiling entirely. PR-08's own criterion treats a recalibration
reporting zero movement as a finding to explain rather than a pass, and D-12 extends that to an upward
move. None of this may be hand-written: this project has already had a hand-corrected generated
artifact silently revert on the next run.

Output: `scripts/compute-ceiling-recalibration.mjs`, its test sibling, the committed
`36-CEILING-RECALIBRATION.md`, and one npm script.

Requirement-tick safety: this plan ticks nothing and edits no requirement or roadmap document. Skip any
end-of-plan `requirements mark-complete` call.
</objective>
