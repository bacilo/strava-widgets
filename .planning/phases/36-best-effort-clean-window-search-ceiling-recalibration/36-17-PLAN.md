<objective>
Choose and enforce one contract for how `contaminated` and `demotion` relate (WR-02), at the writer and in
the independent recount, so the dashboard plan (36-18) and the docblocks state something the producer
actually guarantees.

THE CHOSEN CONTRACT AND WHY. The review offered two options. "Make contaminated-and-demoted impossible by
construction" would require one of two things. Either the writer drops a truthful measurement record (the
original window really did intersect a segment), or it suppresses a guard's judgment of the clean figure.
D-08 already settles the second: the absolute guards evaluate the CLEAN figure, and when that figure fails,
`demotion` is set. Pass 3's ceiling does the same. So the contaminated-plus-demotion pairing is something
the locked design produces, and was real on the archive before the detector fix (`4598855187`@1k, recorded
in 36-11/36-13). The contract is therefore:

- `contaminated` describes which window was measured.
- `demotion` independently says whether that measured figure ranks.
- The only impossible pair is `contaminated` together with an `unreliable-segment` demotion.

D-05's "ranks normally" is read as "ranks normally unless a guard rejects the clean figure", which is
D-08's own precedence.

planner's contract" (relayed by the plan-phase orchestrator after the plan checker raised it). The contract

amendment on PR-07 (amending D-05) in Task 1, before execution judges anything against it. Plan 36-20
