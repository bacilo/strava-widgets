<objective>
Add SEG-06 as the seventh per-activity quality signal, tiered by plan 33-08's constant and
structurally excluded from the severe composite.

Purpose: SEG-06's measured reason for exclusion is the whole point — 699 of 1,874 activities
(37.3%, 32-CALIBRATION.md) carry at least one segment, so folding this into `hasAnySevereSignal`
would roughly triple v2.2's composite figure of record (299 of 1,890, 15.8%) and break its
comparability across milestones. The elevation signal established this exact pattern in Phase 30,
including a guard test that must be watched failing before it is trusted.

Output: a new signal interface and assembler, the shipped constant, the seventh key on
`ActivityQualitySignals`, the mechanical literal sweep that key forces, and the never-widen guard.

Requirement-tick safety: this plan's `requirements` field is a MAPPING of which requirements its
evidence serves, not an authority to tick them. This plan ticks NOTHING in REQUIREMENTS.md and
touches neither the ROADMAP Phase 33 checkbox nor STATE.md's progress block. Any end-of-plan
`requirements mark-complete` invocation must be SKIPPED for SEG-06, PACE-08, PACE-09 and COV-03 —
plan 33-13 alone sets their disposition, and only under the all-rows-PASS rule against rendered
checkpoint evidence. A requirement ticked from an autonomous plan's frontmatter has had to be
reverted on this project before (19-15).
</objective>
