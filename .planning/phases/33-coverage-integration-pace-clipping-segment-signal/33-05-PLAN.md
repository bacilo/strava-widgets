<objective>
Close the fourth silent-inheritance trap — `pace-quality-client.ts`'s hardcoded two-member
`VALID_GAP_KINDS` — and widen the list-row gap-profile badge copy.

Purpose: this is a ONE-LINE fix and the highest-leverage line in the phase. `parseGapInterval`
returns `null` for any `kind` not in the set, and `parsePaceQualityShard` silently skips `null`
entries — no error, no warning, just an undercount. Left unwidened, every browser-side consumer
of a fetched shard's `gapIntervals` is starved of the fourth kind, independently of whether its
own logic is correct, so plan 33-04's itemised evidence line would read zero on every activity
forever.

Output: a three-member parse allowlist proven non-vacuously, and a badge whose text names what
its number counts.
</objective>
