<objective>
Derive D-07's one new constant — the excess-metres fraction above which an activity's
unreliable-segment signal is `severe` — with a non-circularity argument, a D-13 insensitivity
check, and a deliberately circular strawman watched failing first.

Purpose: SEG-06's tier cannot ship before this constant exists, and a constant derived as a raw
tail percentile of the very distribution it thresholds is definitionally unfalsifiable — the top
X% is "severe" by construction regardless of X, so no insensitivity check can meaningfully fail.
Phase 32 rejected exactly that shape twice (L's 7.750 m/s strawman, F's 245.046 m strawman), and
this phase must reject it the same way: by measuring it fail, not by asserting it would.

Output: an `excessMetres` output field, a calibration script and its tests, and
`33-CALIBRATION.md` carrying the derivation, the strawman's failure, the insensitivity table and
the resulting archive-wide severe rate as a reported finding.

   * A run's (or step's) excess metres beyond what the speed limit would allow
   * over its own elapsed seconds. Plan 32-04's single-source audit bans this

used at :528 per BURST run against `params.excessFloorMetres`. D-07 needs excess per MERGED
SEGMENT, over the segment's own elapsed seconds (burst seconds plus any merged gap seconds).

`src/analytics/pace-single-source.test.ts:344` bans the literal `'speedLimitMps * seconds'` in
every `src/**/*.ts` except `unreliable-segments.ts`, `pace-derivation.ts` and `*.test.ts`. That is
why the excess must become an OUTPUT FIELD rather than being recomputed in `pace-quality.ts`:
re-deriving it there would either trip the audit or evade its needle by renaming a variable, and
needle-evasion is the defect the audit exists to catch.

PLANNER DECISION resolving 33-RESEARCH.md Assumption A3 / its Open Question on the minor tier:
D-07 names exactly ONE new constant, and the literal reading that honours it is taken —
`none` = the activity has zero detected segments; `minor` = at least one detected segment with an
excess fraction at or below the severe cut; `severe` = excess fraction above the cut. No second
constant is derived. Rationale: the none/minor boundary is a presence fact, not a magnitude
judgement, so it needs no calibration; and D-06's own measurement (699 of 1,874 activities carry
at least one segment) is precisely why presence must NOT be severe — it says nothing about
whether presence should be silent, and `minor` is the tier that discloses presence without
badging a row.
