<groundwork>
Verified during planning against the shipped files. Both mechanisms are fully diagnosed; the executor applies, reconciles and guards them rather than re-diagnosing them.

**CR-02 — the cascade.** `button { border-radius: var(--radius-control) }` (styles.css:1249-1254) is specificity `0,0,1` and reaches every `.segmented__option`. `.segmented__option` (styles.css:837-843) declares `background`, `color`, `border`, `padding`, `cursor` — and **no `border-radius`**, so it never cancels the baseline. Only `.segmented__option:first-child` and `:last-child` (styles.css:850-856, `0,1,1`) override it. Every middle option therefore gets 4px on all four corners. Plan 19-04 removed `.segmented`'s `overflow: hidden`, so nothing clips them square any more, and `.segmented` declares no background — the page background shows through a notch at every internal join. The governing comment at styles.css:845-849 asserts the opposite ("these two end-child rules reproduce the previous rounded silhouette without clipping anything"); that claim is true for exactly two options.

**The three live 3+-option groups, all in `src/dashboard/views/trends.ts`, all confirmed by reading:**

| Control | Container | Options | Middle options |
|---------|-----------|---------|----------------|
| Trends tab list | `trends.ts:1110`, `role="tablist"`, `className = 'segmented'` | `TREND_TAB_KEYS` = 5 (`trends-logic.ts:20-26`): Volume, Year-over-Year, Cadence & HR, Training Load, Gear | **3** — Year-over-Year, Cadence & HR, Training Load |
| Volume granularity | `trends.ts:595`, `role="group"`, `aria-label="Volume granularity"` | Weekly, Monthly, Yearly (`trends.ts:588-592`) | **1** — Monthly |
| Training-load window | `trends.ts:867`, `role="group"`, `aria-label="Training load window"` | 3mo, 12mo, All (`trends-training-load-logic.ts:158`) | **1** — 12mo |

The fourth `.segmented` in Trends — the Edwards/Banister model group (`trends.ts:811`) — has two options and is unaffected, as is the detail view's x-axis toggle. Those two are the reference controls: if the fix is right, they look exactly as they do today.

The defect is loudest on an active middle option: `.segmented__option--active` fills with `var(--accent-strong)` (styles.css:858-861), so selecting "12mo", "Monthly" or any of the three middle tabs renders a fully-rounded accent pill inside the group. Inactive middles show `--surface`-vs-`--bg` notches — subtle in light, clearly visible in dark (`#242444` on `#1a1a2e`).

**The fix must cancel the mechanism, not work around it.** `.segmented__option { border-radius: 0 }` is `0,1,0`, beats the `0,0,1` baseline, and is itself beaten by the two `0,1,1` end-child rules that follow it — so the end caps keep their radii with no ordering dependency. Do **not** scope or weaken the `button` baseline: reaching every button is D-05's whole point, and removing `border-radius` from it would un-round every class-less button (the three `calendar.ts` sites documented at styles.css:1222-1248) and every input-adjacent control that inherits it.

**CR-03 — the mechanism.** `:disabled, [aria-disabled="true"] { opacity: 0.6 }` (styles.css:1300-1305) and `:focus-visible`'s `box-shadow` ring (styles.css:380-385) land on the **same element**. `opacity` applies to the element's entire rendered output including its `box-shadow`, so the ring composites at 60%.

Calendar rest days are the live case and are *deliberately* focusable: `calendar.ts:118-131` keeps them as real `<button>` elements with **no** `disabled` attribute ("still a real, focusable button for a consistent Tab order"), adds `.calendar-day--rest`, and sets `aria-disabled="true"`. Tab reaches them, `:focus-visible` matches, and the ring paints at 0.6 alpha. Recomputing the file's own numbers (styles.css:346-350, W3C relative luminance, SC 1.4.11 3:1 non-text threshold) with the accent stop blended at 60% over the backdrop:

| Theme | Ring stop | Backdrop | Blended | Contrast | File claims |
|-------|-----------|----------|---------|----------|-------------|
| light | `--accent` `#fc4c02` | `--bg` `#ffffff` | `#fd9467` | **2.19:1** | 3.40:1 |
| dark | `--accent` `#ff6b35` | `--bg` `#1a1a2e` | `#a34b32` | **2.93:1** | 6.02:1 |

Both fail. In a typical month roughly a third to a half of the calendar grid is rest days, so this is the common case, not an edge.

**Reconciling the fix with D-07 — settle this before writing anything.** D-07 requires a disabled treatment of "muted color via `--text-secondary`, `cursor: default`, reduced opacity", and CONTEXT.md § Claude's Discretion explicitly leaves "precise opacity/color values for the D-07 disabled treatment" to judgement. Restoring `opacity: 1` **only while `:focus-visible`** therefore does not contradict D-07: at rest the control is dimmed exactly as D-07 requires, and even while focused it keeps `color: var(--text-secondary)`, keeps `cursor: default`, and is still excluded from the shared hover rule (styles.css:1274-1285 lists both `:disabled` and `[aria-disabled="true"]` in its `:where(:not(...))`). The only state in which the opacity dimming is dropped is the one state in which the element is announcing focus and the ring must be legible.

The alternative — moving the dimming off the button onto its inner content — is rejected: the disabled rule is global and reaches elements with no inner wrapper, and a `> *` variant would not dim bare text nodes. Record that rejection in the comment rather than leaving the reader to wonder.

Specificity: `:disabled:focus-visible` and `[aria-disabled="true"]:focus-visible` are both `0,2,0`, beating the `0,1,0` base rule regardless of order. The `:disabled` arm is symmetric rather than live — a `<button disabled>` is not focusable, so only the `aria-disabled` arm fires today. Keep both, mirroring D-07's dual selector, and say so.
