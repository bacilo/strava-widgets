<objective>
Collapse the two divergent stream-derived pace implementations onto the shared module.

Purpose: PACE-01's whole point is that the chart and the histogram disagree today because they
compute pace differently — a 20s smoothed window versus raw per-sample `dt/dd`. That disagreement
is what produces the phantom fast mode and the 72%-coverage claim on the worked example. After this
plan there is one derivation and both surfaces read it.

Output: `detail-charts-logic.ts` and `detail-zones.ts` become thin consumers; `detail.ts` calls the
shared entry point once per render.
</objective>
