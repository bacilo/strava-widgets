## The one genuine open seam

Phase 16's UAT items 1-3 — light-OS legibility, first-paint white flash, and live OS auto-follow — remain untested. The tempting assumption is that Phases 17 and 18, both human-verified in a real browser on this same shell, discharge them indirectly. The integration check found they do not: both later checkpoints appear to have run from a dark-OS machine toggling the in-page control, and neither validation record mentions a first-paint check or an OS-level appearance change. These three items are exactly as untested now as they were at the end of Phase 16.

This is cosmetic and environmental risk rather than a functional break — but it is real, and it sits in the same phase that once shipped a black page behind a green gate.

## Recommendation

Close the milestone. No requirement is unsatisfied and no flow is broken. Carry the tech-debt block forward: the Phase 16 theme items and the two integration warnings are the items most worth resolving, and none of them blocks a tag.
